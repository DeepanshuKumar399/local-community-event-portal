Community Bulletin

The city council is pleased to announce a series of community events planned for the upcoming months. Residents are encouraged to participate actively and make the most of these opportunities to connect with neighbours and celebrate local culture.

The Annual City Fair returns this August with more stalls, performances, and activities than ever before. Local artisans, food vendors, and performers have been invited to showcase their talents at City Park. Entry is priced at ₹50 per person, with children under 10 admitted free.

Sports Day will be held at the Sports Complex on 22nd August. Events include athletics, team sports, and fun games for all age groups. Participation is free and open to all residents.

Cultural Night at Town Hall promises an evening of music, dance, and drama performed by local artists. Tickets are priced at ₹100 and can be booked online through this portal.
